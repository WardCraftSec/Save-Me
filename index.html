Step 1: Make a free account

You need a free NextDNS account. It's like a free email address, but for your internet settings.

Go to nextdns.io and tap Sign up. You can use any email — it doesn't have to be a real one you check often.

Once you're in, you'll see a long string of numbers. That's your profile ID. Keep it somewhere safe.

Stuck? Book a free 5-min call or close this page — your progress isn't lost.

Step 2: Turn on the filters

NextDNS has pre-made filter lists. You don't need to build your own — just switch them on.

In your NextDNS dashboard, go to Filters. Turn on:

• Blocklist for porn (or whichever category you need)
• Blocklist for ads (optional, but recommended)

Tap Save when you're done.

Stuck? Phones look different. You can: scroll back up / book a free 5-min call / close this page, your progress isn't lost.

Step 3: Turn it on in your phone

A setting called "Private DNS" — it tells your phone to route everything through NextDNS.

Android: Settings → Network & Internet → Private DNS → Enter provider hostname → type dns.nextdns.io → Save

iPhone: This step works differently on iOS. Go to nextdns.io and follow their iOS guide (you'll install a small profile).

Stuck? Phones look different. You can: scroll back up / book a free 5-min call / close this page, your progress isn't lost.

Step 4: Check it's working

One quick test to confirm everything is connected.

Open test.nextdns.io in your browser.

✅ Green tick = you're set.
❌ Red cross = go back to Step 3, or I can help.

Stuck? If it's red, the most common fix is re-entering the hostname in Step 3. Make sure there are no spaces.

Step 5: Read your logs

Your NextDNS dashboard shows every site your phone tried to visit. This is useful to know what's being blocked.

In your dashboard, tap Logs. You'll see a list of domains with timestamps. If you see something you don't recognise, you can check it below in the Site Checker.

Tip: tap any domain in the log to copy it, then paste it into the checker.

Stuck? The logs can look overwhelming. You only need to look at entries that surprise you. Everything else is normal background traffic.

Step 6: Block specific sites

If you see a specific domain you want blocked (not just a category), you can add it to a denylist.

In your dashboard: Filters → Denylist → paste the domain → Save.

That domain will now be blocked on any device using your NextDNS profile.

Stuck? You don't need to do this step right now. You can add domains to the denylist at any time.
