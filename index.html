Save Me

This helps you set up a filter on your phone so that certain sites and apps can't load. It takes about 5 minutes. You don't need to know any tech.

This site doesn't track you. Nothing you do here is recorded. You can close this tab at any time.

Create your filter account

First, we need to make a free account at NextDNS. It's the service that does the filtering.

  1. Open this link in your browser: my.nextdns.io
  2. Tap Sign Up
  3. Use any email. You don't need to share your real name.

When you're in, you'll see a dashboard. That's where we'll go next.

Turn on the filters

Now we turn on the filters that block the stuff you want to avoid.

  1. On the dashboard, tap Filters (left sidebar)
  2. Under Blocklists, tick: OISD (ads & trackers), AdGuard DNS Filter
  3. Under Privacy, tick: Adult
  4. Tap Save

The filter is now active.

Connect your phone

Now we point your phone at the filter.

Android setup

  1. Open your phone's Settings app
  2. Search for Private DNS (or Network & Internet → Private DNS)
  3. Tap Private DNS provider hostname
  4. Back on NextDNS dashboard, tap Setup → Android
  5. Copy the address it shows (looks like abcd1234.dns.nextdns.io)
  6. Paste it into the box on your phone
  7. Tap Save

Your phone is now filtering.

iPhone setup

  1. On your iPhone, open Safari
  2. Go to: apple.nextdns.io/abcd1234 (replace with your ID from the dashboard)
  3. Tap Download Profile
  4. Open Settings → tap Profile Downloaded at the top
  5. Tap Install → enter passcode → Install again

Your iPhone is now filtering.

Check it's working

Let's confirm the filter is actually on.

  1. Open your browser
  2. Go to: test.nextdns.io
  3. If it shows your config ID, you're good.

If not, go back and double-check the address you entered.

Reading your logs

NextDNS keeps a log of every site your phone tries to reach. You can look at it any time.

  1. Go back to my.nextdns.io
  2. Tap Logs (left sidebar)
  3. Red = blocked. No colour = allowed through.
  4. Tap the ⓘ next to any red entry to see why it was blocked.

You'll often see apps contacting trackers in the background. That's normal. The filter is doing its job.

Blocking a specific site

If there's a specific site the filter isn't catching, you can block it by name.

  1. On the NextDNS dashboard, tap Filters
  2. Scroll to Denylist
  3. Type the site's address (e.g. example.com)
  4. Tap Add

It'll be blocked within seconds. Remove it any time by tapping the × next to it.

You're set up

The filter runs in the background. You don't need to do anything else.

If something breaks, go to Filters → Allowlist and add that site. It'll come right back.

You can always turn this off. Settings → Private DNS → set back to Automatic.

You've got this.
